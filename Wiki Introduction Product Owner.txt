Wiki Introduction: Product Owner

Hello everyone, my name is ISULI Vidara, and I am excited to serve as the Product Owner for our Scrum team. My primary responsibility is to maximize the value of our product by managing and prioritizing the Product Backlog, ensuring we always work on the features that deliver the most impact to our users and business. I will also act as the voice of the customer, translating stakeholder needs into clear, actionable user stories so the team has a shared understanding of what to build and why it matters. Additionally, I will collaborate closely with you during Sprint Planning and reviews to refine requirements, answer questions quickly, and remove any ambiguity that could slow us down. My goal is to ensure we build the right product, not just build the product right. I am looking forward to collaborating with all of you and building something we can all be proud of.


## Reflection

Clearly defining my role as Product Owner at the start helps prevent future misunderstandings by establishing who owns backlog prioritization and decision-making authority, so the team knows exactly where to bring questions about scope and value. One potential conflict this introduction could help avoid is the classic tension between the team and stakeholders over shifting priorities mid-Sprint, because it sets the expectation that all new requests flow through me for proper evaluation and backlog placement rather than disrupting the team directly.
